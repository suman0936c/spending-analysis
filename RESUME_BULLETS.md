**Personal Expense and Spending Behavior Analysis | Excel, MySQL, SQL**

- Analyzed 119 personal-expense transactions from December 2025 using MySQL and Excel to surface category, payment-mode, and day-level spending patterns.
- Built an Excel dashboard with KPI cards and trend, category, and weekday-versus-weekend visualizations; reported INR 14,533 in total spend and an INR 484.43 average daily spend.
- Wrote reusable MySQL queries using aggregations, CTEs, and window functions to identify Food as the largest category (42.5% of spend) and flag the INR 2,090 highest-spend day.

**One-line version:** Built an Excel and MySQL personal-expense analytics project, analyzing 119 transactions to identify category concentration, daily outliers, and payment behavior.
